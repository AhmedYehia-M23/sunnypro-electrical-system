# Electrical Design Ownership Statement

The SunnyPro machine was developed as a university group project. I was **solely responsible for the electrical architecture and all KiCad schematic design contained in this source package**.

I personally created the hierarchical KiCad schematics, electrical architecture, MCU I/O planning, power-distribution and protection design, temperature-sensing and heater-control interfaces, motor/encoder interfaces, load-cell measurement interfaces, actuator drivers, component selection, power calculations and electrical BOM work represented here.

The electrical schematics in this package were not supplied as a pre-designed course schematic.

This ownership statement applies specifically to the **electrical architecture and schematic design documented in this repository**; it does not claim authorship of unrelated mechanical, software or other team-developed parts of the overall SunnyPro machine.
